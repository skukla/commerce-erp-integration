Clickable mockups of the redesigned ERP Integration page inside Commerce Admin. Static data only. On each page, the purple Mockup switch in the bottom-right corner shows the page with problems: Contoso ERP in maintenance, and orders and ERP updates that did not get through.

OverviewIs it working, and does anything need me? One card per ERP, what needs attention, today’s counts, and one search box. ActivityEverything that crossed, newest first, grouped by day. Filter by ERP, type or problems; click a row for the order’s trace or a side-by-side lookup. SettingsPick All ERPs or one ERP, and Default Config or a website. Only the settings that apply there are shown.

What’s covered (the integration already has this data)

  • Every setting the integration defines: sending orders, holding them while an ERP is offline, the status when an ERP confirms, sales organization and its name, which products each ERP owns (all, inventory sources, or a product attribute), the sources, the attribute, and the order-number prefix. Each is shown where it applies: for all ERPs or per ERP, at Default Config or per website.
  • Each ERP’s name, ID (what products’ erp_owner holds), kind, address, and whether it has its own credential. Never the secret.
  • Each ERP’s health: connected, or not reachable and why (for example a maintenance window); its products, business partners and sales orders; updates waiting to send; when Demo Builder last filled and emptied it.
  • Every kind of Activity record, with its result: orders sent; shipments, invoices, holds and cancellations made in Commerce and sent to the ERP; and, from the ERP, order status, shipment, invoice, cancel, credit hold, credit limit, company block, price, stock and customer prices. Results: Sent, Applied, Sending, Waiting for the ERP, Not sent, Not applied yet, Refused by Commerce, Done. Also the Demo reset line, with tries and Retry.
  • The order trace: placed, each part sent or waiting (and why), what each ERP did, each ERP update applied or not, and Retry or Re-send.
  • The product and company side-by-side lookups, with every row the lookup returns.
  • The scheduled price publish (last run, last change, next run) and the Move stock result.
  • Needs attention, “last update from it” and today’s counts. The page works these out from the Activity records it already reads (up to 100 records, kept 14 days).

Needs new data or a change

  • Order status when the ERP confirms, as a dropdown. Today it is typed in. A dropdown needs the list of Commerce statuses assigned to the Pending state.
  • Searching companies by name. The lookup takes the Commerce company id. The mockup accepts “Kukla Studios” too, but a name search would need a new company search.
  • Company names in Activity rows. ERP records hold the company or customer id (“company 4: credit limit 25000”). The mockup shows names; the page would have to look each one up.
  • Which ERP a change made in Commerce went to. Shipments, invoices, holds and cancellations made in Commerce Admin are recorded without naming the ERP. So they show “ERP not named” and appear only under All ERPs.
  • Products and company changes sent from Commerce to the ERP. These are sent, but not recorded in Activity, so they are not shown.
  • Today’s counts split per ERP. Stock records that list several lines do not name an ERP, so a per-ERP split would miss them. The counts are shown for all ERPs only.
  • Settings wording. The integration’s own labels and help text say “organisation” and “cancelled”. The mockup uses American English; the code would change to match.
